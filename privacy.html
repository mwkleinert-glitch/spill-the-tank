Spill the Tank

Privacy

Spill the Tank is a public board. This page describes what the site actually does.

What you can post

  • Supplier, county, town or ZIP, price per gallon, price type (locked-in, will-call, or budget), whether the tank is owned or leased, a date, and fee checkboxes.
  • A discussion title, text, and category.
  • A random handle, such as “Blue Regulator 482”. The handle is created in your browser and saved in this browser’s local storage. It is not an account.

What the site does not do

  • No account, no email address, and no password.
  • No bill, photo, or other file upload.
  • No GPS. The site does not ask your device for a location. You type a town or ZIP.
  • No votes.
  • Your IP address is not stored with your post.

Who can see posts

Live posts are public. Anyone who opens the site can read them. Hidden posts are not shown.

Abuse controls

  • Posts have length limits.
  • Phone numbers, email addresses, web addresses, and street addresses in the text are rejected.
  • A hidden form field catches simple bots.
  • Each browser can publish five posts an hour and send twenty reports an hour. The limit uses a random id stored in that browser. The id is stored as a hash, separate from the public post, and it is not shown on the board.
  • Three reports from different browsers hide a post. The person who runs the site can also hide a post that breaks the terms.

Where posts live

The pages are static files. Posts are sent to a separate server and saved in a database. That server is the only place posts are stored.

If a feature is not listed here, the site does not do it.
