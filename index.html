Locked-in / Prebuy vs. Will-call vs. Budget

Locked-in or prebuy prices are rates you agree to in advance (often in spring or summer) for a set volume or period. Will-call prices are what you pay when you simply call for a delivery with no prior agreement. Budget plans typically spread estimated annual costs into equal monthly payments, with a true-up at the end.

When a locked-in or prebuy contract ends mid-winter, remaining deliveries usually switch to the current will-call rate, which is frequently higher. The advertised locked-in price is almost always the base per-gallon rate only.

Common add-ons to ask about

  • Sales tax
  • Delivery or service fees
  • Environmental / hazmat fees
  • Tank rental or lease fees
  • Minimum-delivery charges
  • After-hours or emergency delivery fees
  • Leak check fees
  • Tank removal or pickup fees

These can add significantly to the total cost. Always ask for the all-in price or a full fee schedule.

Questions to ask your propane company before you sign

  • Is this price locked for the full season or a specific volume? What happens if I need more?
  • What is the current will-call price, and how often does it change?
  • Which fees are included in the quoted price? Which are extra?
  • Is the tank owned or leased? What is the monthly or annual lease cost?
  • Are there minimum-delivery, after-hours, or leak-check charges?
  • What is the process and cost if I switch suppliers or remove the tank later?
  • Can I see a recent sample bill that shows every line item?

This is general information only. Pricing structures vary by company and region. Always confirm details directly with your supplier.
